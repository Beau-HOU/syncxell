1. Executive Summary & Company
Profile
1.1 Company Description
SYNCXELL LLC is a full-spectrum IT solutions provider specializing in delivering secure,
scalable, and mission-critical technology services to the US Government and large enterprises.
With a foundation built on a diverse and highly-skilled team, our expertise spans the entire
technology stack, from application development and cloud infrastructure to robust cybersecurity
and data analytics.
Our professionals bring firsthand experience from high-stakes environments, including prior
work for the US and French governments, major airlines, and leading technology companies.
This unique background gives us a deep understanding of complex operational needs, strict
compliance requirements, and the necessity for uninterrupted, high-performance systems.
We are committed to leveraging this expertise to help our clients modernize their IT
environments, enhance operational efficiency, and secure their digital assets in today's evolving
threat landscape.
1.2 Document Purpose
This Cahier des Charges serves as the definitive structural and functional blueprint for the
engineering of the SYNCXELL LLC corporate digital platform. The objective is to build a highperformance web application that captures high-value contracts from federal and municipal
governments, as well as highly regulated private sectors (banking, legal). It outlines the
architecture, UX/UI requirements, service representations, and robust security protocols
necessary to reflect the elite technological pedigree of the company.

Financial CIOs are looking for hybrid cloud solutions, secure data warehousing, predictive
analytics for fraud detection, and disaster recovery. The website must detail our AES-256
encryption standards, data isolation techniques, and regulatory compliance mapping.

Corporate legal entities require ultra-secure document management systems, bespoke software
application development, and resilient network architectures. The messaging for this persona
emphasizes absolute data confidentiality, high-availability networks for constant global access,
and agile development pipelines to modernize their internal operations.

14. Web Platform Security &
Compliance Mandates
The corporate website of a cybersecurity and IT solutions provider must itself be an
impenetrable fortress, serving as a live demonstration of SYNCXELL's capabilities.
14.1 Infrastructure Security
The hosting environment must enforce HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), permitting only
TLS 1.3 connections. A robust Web Application Firewall (WAF) must be deployed to
automatically filter out DDoS attempts, SQL injection, and Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) attacks.
14.2 Section 508 Accessibility Compliance
Because the target audience includes US Federal agencies, the website's front-end must strictly
adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 guidelines. This entails perfect color contrast ratios,
comprehensive ARIA labeling for screen readers, complete keyboard navigability, and dynamic
text scaling.

16. Recommended Technical Stack for
the Platform
To deliver on the performance, security, and scalability requirements outlined in this document,
the following technology stack is mandated for the development of the SYNCXELL website.
16.1 Front-End Architecture
A modern JavaScript framework such as Next.js (React) or Nuxt.js (Vue) is required. These
frameworks provide Server-Side Rendering (SSR) or Static Site Generation (SSG), resulting in
lightning-fast page load times, superior SEO indexing (vital for procurement searches), and a
highly secure front-end separated from the database layer.
16.2 Headless CMS Backend
A Headless Content Management System (such as Sanity, Strapi, or Contentful) should be
utilized. This allows the SYNCXELL marketing team to update services, publish case studies,
and manage the bilingual (English/French) content easily, without exposing a traditional backend
(like WordPress) to massive vulnerability scanning.

17. Quality Assurance & Launch
Criteria
Before the SYNCXELL corporate platform is transitioned to a live production environment, it
must pass a rigorous matrix of validation gates.
17.1 Security Penetration Testing
A third-party or isolated internal team must conduct dynamic application security testing (DAST)
and static code analysis (SAST) to ensure the encrypted intake forms and CMS APIs are fully
hardened against exploitation.
17.2 Performance Profiling
The platform must achieve a Google Lighthouse score of 95+ across Performance, Accessibility,
Best Practices, and SEO. Page Time-to-Interactive (TTI) must be under 1.5 seconds on
broadband connections.
17.3 Final Handover
Upon successful QA, the development team will provide comprehensive documentation covering
the deployment pipeline, CMS operational guidelines, and WAF configuration states to the
SYNCXELL executive board.
